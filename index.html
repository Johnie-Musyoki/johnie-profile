About

I work in cyber security analysis, with most of my time spent on vulnerability assessment, penetration testing and security advisory. I take systems apart to understand how they actually behave, then document what needs to change and why it matters.

My background is in information technology, and I moved into security because the interesting problems sit where systems, people and process meet. I'm comfortable in a terminal, in front of a client, and in the gap between a finding and a fix.

Outside work I mentor students entering the field, lead a student security community, and build small tools to solve problems I keep running into.
