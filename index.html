Common questions

What does a vulnerability assessment cover?

Testing across web applications, APIs, network infrastructure and mobile platforms, using both black-box and white-box methods. Findings are ranked by business risk rather than raw scanner severity, so the report leads with what actually matters to the system owner.

Do you provide penetration testing services in Kenya?

Yes. The service covers web, API, network and mobile targets, mapped to OWASP Top 10 and OWASP ASVS, and delivered with a remediation-focused report and stakeholder walkthrough.

Can you help with ISO 27001 or PCI DSS compliance?

Yes. That work sits under security advisory: IT audit and control assessment, information security governance, and risk and compliance advisory across ISO 27001, PCI DSS and data protection obligations.

What happens during an incident response engagement?

Four stages: scoping and containment, evidence preservation, root-cause analysis, and post-incident hardening so the same path is harder to walk a second time.

Where are you based and what work do you take on?

Based in Nairobi, Kenya. The focus is security advisory, vulnerability assessment and penetration testing work.
